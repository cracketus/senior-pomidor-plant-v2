About

Senior Pomidor started as a real tomato-growing experiment on a Vienna balcony, but the goal is broader: build an open, reproducible AI core for acting in the physical world. The system separates observation, state estimation, prediction, decision-making, safety guardrails, execution, and evaluation. It is designed to work with different sensors and existing farm systems instead of a closed hardware stack, while keeping the full state → decision → action → outcome chain traceable for research and replay. AI models can support analysis and prediction; physical authority stays explicit, bounded, and testable.
